Multi-Class Sentiment Classification

Problem
Short social-media posts contain mixed emotional signals, making negative, neutral, and positive sentiment difficult to distinguish.
Approach
Mapped 13 emotions across 40,000 posts into three classes, built a 42,633-feature bag-of-words representation, and compared Naive Bayes, logistic regression, and TextBlob on an 80/20 split.
Impact
Logistic regression achieved the highest weighted F1 (0.558 versus 0.507 for Naive Bayes and 0.389 for TextBlob), with neutral-class F1 improving from 0.187 to 0.406 over Naive Bayes.
